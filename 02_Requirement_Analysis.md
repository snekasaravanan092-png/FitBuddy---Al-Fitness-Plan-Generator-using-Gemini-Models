# FitBuddy — Requirement Analysis

## Page 4 — Problem Statement

Personal trainers and dieticians can be costly and may not be available to everyone. Generic online fitness plans often ignore differences in age, body profile, fitness goal, experience level, equipment, and food habits.

Beginners can also face confusion because online information may contain many different recommendations.

### Identified Problems
- High cost of personalised guidance.
- Limited access to trainers or dieticians.
- Generic plans may not match the user's profile.
- Conflicting information can confuse beginners.
- Users may lose motivation when a plan does not feel personalised.

---

## Page 5 — Need, Impact and Solution

### Need
A fast, accessible and personalised fitness-plan generator that can produce a structured plan from basic user information.

### Impact
Without suitable guidance, users may lose motivation, follow routines that do not match their level, or increase the risk of exercising incorrectly.

### Proposed Solution
FitBuddy uses Gemini AI to create a custom workout and meal plan from the user's profile.

### Key Value
- Profile-based personalisation.
- Day-wise workout structure.
- Diet preference matching.
- Fast AI-generated output.
- Simple presentation for beginners.

---

## Page 6 — Problem-to-Solution Mapping

| Problem | FitBuddy Solution | Gemini Role |
|---|---|---|
| High trainer cost | Free AI-generated plans | Acts as a virtual coaching layer |
| Generic plans | Plan built from user profile | Personalises output |
| Confusing information | Simple day-wise format | Structures the response |
| Diet mismatch | Vegetarian / non-vegetarian / vegan / eggetarian choices | Suggests suitable meals |

### Target Users
- Students and beginners
- Working professionals
- Home-workout users

### Constraints
- Internet connection is required.
- Gemini API availability/limits affect generation.
- Generated plans are guidance and not medical advice.
