# FitBuddy — Brainstorming & Ideation

## Page 1 — Abstract

**Project:** FitBuddy — AI Fitness Plan Generator

FitBuddy is an AI-powered fitness plan generator built with Google Gemini models. Many people cannot afford a personal trainer or dietician, while generic online plans may not suit an individual's age, body profile, goal, activity level, or food preference.

The user enters details such as age, height, weight, goal, activity level/fitness level, equipment, and diet preference. FitBuddy uses Gemini to generate a personalised workout and meal plan in seconds.

### Core Idea
- Make personalised fitness guidance accessible.
- Use a simple web form so beginners can provide their details easily.
- Generate a day-wise workout and meal plan.
- Present the result in a clean, readable format.

---

## Page 2 — Objective and Outcome

### Objective
Give users instant, free and personalised fitness guidance through a simple web application.

### Approach
FitBuddy combines:
1. A simple web input form.
2. Profile validation and basic calculations.
3. A Gemini API request with a structured prompt.
4. A day-wise workout and meal-plan display.

### Expected Outcome
The project aims to provide easy-to-follow plans that help users stay consistent with fitness activities while keeping the interface simple and accessible.

## Page 3 — Ideas Priority

### High Priority — Phase 1
- AI workout plan
- AI diet plan
- Simple input form

### Medium Priority — Phase 2
- BMI and calorie calculator
- Multi-language support
- Download plan as PDF

### Low Priority / Future
- Progress tracker
- Chat with AI coach
- Wearable integration

### Why This Order?
The AI workout/diet generation and input form provide the core value of FitBuddy. BMI/calorie features add useful supporting information. Progress tracking, AI chat, and wearable integration require additional development effort and are therefore future enhancements.
